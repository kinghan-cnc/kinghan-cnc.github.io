Understanding C3604 Brass Alloy

C3604 is a free-machining brass alloy containing approximately 60% copper and 40% zinc, with small additions of lead for enhanced machinability. This composition provides excellent turning, drilling, and threading characteristics while maintaining good strength and corrosion resistance.

Swiss Advantage: Our Swiss lathes achieve mirror-like finishes on C3604 brass with Ra values as low as 0.1μm, often eliminating secondary finishing operations.

Key Properties of C3604 Brass

Mechanical Properties

  • Tensile Strength: 340-450 MPa
  • Yield Strength: 125-200 MPa
  • Elongation: 15-25%
  • Hardness: 75-95 HB
  • Density: 8.5 g/cm³

Machining Characteristics

  • Excellent machinability: 90% rating (compared to free-cutting steel)
  • Self-lubricating: Lead content provides natural lubrication
  • Chip breaking: Forms short, easily managed chips
  • Tool life: Extended cutting tool life due to low work hardening
