New eBook: The Complete Guide to Sourcing CNC Parts from Asia

Published February 23, 2026 · By KingHan Precision CNC · 5 min read

← Back to Blog

We're excited to announce the release of our new Kindle eBook: The Complete Guide to Sourcing CNC Parts from Asia: What Every Engineer and Buyer Needs to Know. Priced at just $9.99, this book distills years of real-world manufacturing experience into a practical, actionable guide for engineers, procurement managers, and business owners.

Why We Wrote This Book

Sourcing precision CNC parts from Asia can be incredibly rewarding — lower costs, access to advanced manufacturing capabilities, and scalable production capacity. But it can also be a minefield of quality issues, communication breakdowns, IP risks, and hidden costs if you don't know what you're doing.

As a Taiwan-based CNC precision parts supplier with years of experience serving international clients, we've seen every mistake in the book. We wrote this guide so you don't have to learn the hard way.

What You'll Learn

📖 Inside the Book

  • Choosing the Right Country: Comparing China, Taiwan, Vietnam, India, and other Asian manufacturing hubs — strengths, weaknesses, and best-fit scenarios
  • Supplier Evaluation Framework: How to vet CNC machine shops, verify capabilities, and avoid scams
  • Quality Control Strategies: Setting up inspection protocols, understanding certifications (ISO 9001, IATF 16949, AS9100), and managing tolerances across borders
  • Cost Negotiation: Understanding pricing structures, tooling costs, MOQs, and how to get the best value without sacrificing quality
  • Logistics & Shipping: Incoterms, customs, lead time management, and packaging requirements for precision parts
  • Communication Best Practices: Technical drawing standards, language tips, and how to build strong supplier relationships remotely
  • IP Protection: NDAs, contracts, and practical strategies to protect your designs
  • Real Case Studies: Lessons from actual sourcing projects — what went right, what went wrong, and what we'd do differently

Who Is This Book For?

  • Design engineers who need to source custom CNC parts for prototypes or production
  • Procurement managers evaluating Asian suppliers for the first time
  • Startup founders looking to manufacture hardware products cost-effectively
  • Supply chain professionals diversifying their supplier base across Asia
  • Anyone considering outsourcing CNC machining to Asia

Why $9.99 Is a Bargain
