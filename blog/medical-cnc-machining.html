What is Medical-Grade CNC Machining?

Medical-grade CNC machining refers to the precision manufacturing of components specifically designed for medical applications. This specialized form of machining requires adherence to strict quality standards, biocompatibility requirements, and exceptional precision tolerances. Unlike conventional manufacturing, medical CNC machining must comply with regulations such as FDA requirements, ISO 13485 standards, and other international medical device regulations.

Key Fact: Medical device components often require tolerances as tight as ±0.002mm to ±0.005mm, with surface finishes that meet biocompatibility standards for direct human contact.

Critical Requirements for Medical CNC Machining

Precision and Tolerances

Medical devices require exceptional precision to ensure proper functionality and patient safety. Our Swiss-type CNC lathes deliver tolerances of ±0.005mm, meeting the demanding requirements of medical applications. This precision is crucial for components such as:

  • Surgical instrument tips and cutting edges
  • Implant components that must fit precisely with human anatomy
  • Medical device housings that protect sensitive electronics
  • Micro-components for minimally invasive surgical tools
